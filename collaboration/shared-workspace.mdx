A [**workspace**](/core-concepts/workspace) is where a group thinks together in PeerNotes — a seminar, a research group, a knowledge team, a book club. Each member captures privately inside it; what the group shares is the developed thinking. One home, two layers: yours, and the team's. This guide is about working in one as a team; for the settings that shape it, see [Workspace settings](/configuration/workspace-settings).

## Private by default, shared when you choose

Every [thought](/core-concepts/thoughts), [note](/core-concepts/notes), and [source](/core-concepts/sources) you create is **private to you** until you share it. Sharing is a per-item toggle — **Share with workspace** — and it works the same way for all three:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Private" icon="lock">
    Only you can see it. New thoughts, notes, and sources start here.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Shared with the workspace" icon="users">
    Every member can read it in the **Shared** view of that feed and comment on it. Toggle it back off any time to make it private again.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Set a different starting point per type in [Preferences → Sharing](/configuration/user-preferences#sharing) — for example, share every new note automatically but keep thoughts private. Whatever the default, you can still change any individual item afterwards.

[Notes](/core-concepts/notes) go one step further: add [collaborators](/collaboration/real-time-editing) to invite specific people to edit a note with you.

This split is what makes PeerNotes work: capture stays honest because it starts private, and the shared layer stays high-signal because everything in it was deliberately shared.

## Members and administrators

Workspaces have two roles:

- **Members** capture their own thoughts, collaborate on notes they're invited to, and read everything shared with the workspace.
- **Administrators** manage the workspace itself — its settings, members, and topics.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a workspace">
    Give it a name, and optionally seed it with the topics you know you'll be thinking about.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite members by email">
    Add teammates from [Workspace settings → People](/configuration/workspace-settings#people) — you can invite members and add topics anytime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add administrators (optional)">
    Share the admin role with co-organizers who should manage members and settings alongside you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Navigate the workspace

- **Sidebar feeds** — Thoughts, Notes, and Sources each have their own feed, so you always know which layer you're looking at. Each feed has a **Shared** view showing what the team has shared.
- **Search** — press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl K** (Windows) to search across your workspace from anywhere.
- **Topics** — browse the Topics page to see the workspace's themes and dive into everything on a subject.
- **Chat** — ask the [Peer Agent](/core-concepts/peer-agent) about anything in the workspace, or tag it in a comment on any thought, note, or source.

## Multiple workspaces

You can belong to several workspaces — say, one for your team and one for a reading group — and switch between them from the workspace switcher. Each workspace is fully separate: its own members, topics, content, and chat.

<Tip>
  **Treat shared notes as the workspace's record.** When thinking matures — a decision, a finding, a synthesis worth keeping — share it. Over time the Shared view of the Notes feed becomes the knowledge base a new member can read to understand how the group thinks.
</Tip>
