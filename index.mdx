**The intelligent workspace for your team.** Where thoughts become team knowledge.

Most note apps help you *store* ideas. PeerNotes is built for the harder part: reacting to what you read, connecting those reactions over time, and sharpening your thinking against teammates and an AI that works from your own material.

<Frame caption="The PeerNotes workspace — a team's Thoughts feed alongside the Peer Agent, synthesizing patterns across customer interviews">
  ![The PeerNotes workspace on desktop](/images/demo-desktop.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  **Research preview, open to teams.** PeerNotes is free while in preview — no card required. [Get started](https://peernotes.io) or [see every way to reach your workspace](https://get.peernotes.io/download).
</Info>

## How PeerNotes works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture it">
    Clip a page, dictate a thought, or jot a quick note. Everything lands in your workspace, ready to build on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Develop it together">
    Teammates and the Peer Agent weigh in right alongside your notes, sharpening the idea together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it clearly">
    Turn on **Share with workspace** so everyone's on the same page. Read to catch up, or listen on the go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New here? Start with [Account setup](/getting-started/account-setup), [Install PeerNotes](/getting-started/install-extension), then walk through [Creating your first note](/guides/first-note).
