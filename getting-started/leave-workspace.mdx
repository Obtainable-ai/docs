Leaving a workspace removes you from that one workspace. You stay signed in, your account stays open, and your other workspaces aren't affected. To delete your account entirely, see [Delete your account](/getting-started/delete-account).

## Leave a workspace

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web app">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Switch to the workspace">
        Open the workspace you want to leave.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Select your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Leave">
        Under **Your settings**, choose **Leave workspace**, then select the **Leave** button.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile app (iOS and Android)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Switch to the workspace">
        Open the workspace you want to leave.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Leave">
        Go to the **You** tab and tap **Leave workspace** under **Your settings**, then confirm.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  If you're the workspace's only admin, make someone else an admin in [Workspace admin → People](/features/workspace-settings#people) first. If you're its only member, [delete the workspace](/features/workspace-settings#delete-a-workspace) instead.
</Note>

## What happens when you leave

- The workspace drops out of your switcher and you lose access to it.
- Everything you created there stays in the workspace, under your name, so your teammates keep their work.
- A workspace admin can add you back.
- Claude and other AI agents connected to your account lose access to the workspace too.
- If it was your last workspace, you stay signed in and see the welcome screen, where you can create a new workspace.
