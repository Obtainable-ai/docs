PeerNotes runs entirely in your browser, so getting started takes just a few minutes — create an account, set up a workspace, and invite your team.

<Info>
  PeerNotes is in **research preview**, open to teams and **free while in preview — no card required**. See [get.peernotes.io/download](https://get.peernotes.io/download) for every way to reach your workspace.
</Info>

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Open [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io) and select **Continue with Google**. Google is the only sign-in method — there's no separate signup or password. Use the Google account you want associated with your work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    On your first sign-in, PeerNotes carries over your name and photo from Google. Add your role so teammates can recognise you during real-time collaboration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or join a workspace">
    You can either **create a new workspace** for your team or **join an existing one** you've been invited to.

    - To create a workspace, enter a name and optionally upload a logo, then invite teammates by their Google email address.
    - If a teammate has already added you, the workspace appears when you sign in — accept it to jump straight in.
  </Step>
</Steps>
