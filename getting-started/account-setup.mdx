PeerNotes runs entirely in your browser, so getting started takes just a few minutes — create an account, set up a workspace, and invite your team.

<Info>
  PeerNotes is in **research preview**, open to teams and **free while in preview — no card required**. See [get.peernotes.io/download](https://get.peernotes.io/download) for every way to reach your workspace.
</Info>

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to peernotes.io">
    Open [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io) and choose **Get started**. Sign up with your email address, or use the Google or GitHub buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    On your first login, PeerNotes prompts you to complete your profile. Add your name, a profile photo, and your role so teammates can recognise you during real-time collaboration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or join a workspace">
    You can either **create a new workspace** for your team or **join an existing one** using an invite link shared by a teammate.

    - To create a workspace, enter a name and optionally upload a logo, then invite teammates by email.
    - To join an existing workspace, open the invite link your teammate shared — it drops you straight into the workspace after a single confirmation step.
  </Step>
</Steps>
