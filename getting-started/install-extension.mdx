The PeerNotes workspace runs in any browser — there's **nothing to install** to get started. Everything below is an optional add-on that brings capture closer to wherever your thinking happens.

<Info>
  [**get.peernotes.io/download**](https://get.peernotes.io/download) is the always-current list of everywhere PeerNotes runs. This page is a short summary.
</Info>

## In your browser

Open [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io) and you're in — the whole workspace, no download.

Add the **clipper** and the page you're reading becomes a [source](/core-concepts/sources), highlights included:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc & Opera" icon="chrome" href="https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ojongipbblbjdoidjgmefhdhngmimhbi">
    Install the PeerNotes clipper from the Chrome Web Store — one click, with automatic updates. Works in every Chromium-based browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Firefox & Safari" icon="firefox">
    The clipper is coming to Firefox and Safari. Until then, use a Chromium-based browser or clip from your phone.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

After installing, pin the clipper to your toolbar and click it to sign in to your workspace. See [Using the web clipper](/features/web-clipper) for the full workflow.

You can also share a link or screenshot into PeerNotes from any app's share sheet, or dictate a thought on your way out of a meeting — it lands in the same workspace your teammates pick up from the browser.

## In your AI assistant

Connect PeerNotes to an assistant that supports remote [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) — Claude, and a growing list of others — and it answers from your workspace's notes, sources, and topics instead of the open web.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your workspace address">
    `https://api.peernotes.io/mcp`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it as a connector">
    Paste the address where your assistant asks for a remote MCP server or connector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with PeerNotes">
    Authenticate with your PeerNotes account — no API keys to manage. Your assistant only ever sees the workspaces your account can already open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For Claude Desktop or Claude Code, add the server to your config file instead:

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "peernotes": {
      "url": "https://api.peernotes.io/mcp"
    }
  }
}
```

In Claude Desktop, that's **Settings › Developer › Edit config**, then restart Claude. Full instructions live at [get.peernotes.io/download](https://get.peernotes.io/download).
