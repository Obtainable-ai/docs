This page explains how to permanently delete your PeerNotes account and what happens to your data. It applies to the PeerNotes web app and the PeerNotes mobile apps for iOS and Android, published by Obtainable LLC (DBA PeerNotes).

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is permanent and can't be undone. To stop using just one workspace and keep your account, [leave the workspace](/getting-started/leave-workspace) instead.
</Warning>

## Before you start

- **Workspaces where you're the only member and an admin are deleted**, with everything in them. PeerNotes names them before you confirm. The PeerNotes Agent doesn't count as a member.
- **If you're the only admin of a workspace that has other members**, make someone else an admin or delete that workspace first. PeerNotes lists these workspaces after you select **Delete account**, with a link to each fix.
- **Want a copy of your content?** [Email us](mailto:info@peernotes.io) before you delete your account. We can't recover it afterwards.

## Delete your account in the app

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web app">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Account">
        Sign in at [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io), select your name at the bottom of the sidebar, and choose **Account**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Delete account">
        Select **Delete account**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Type **delete my account**, then select **Delete account**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile app (iOS and Android)">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Account">
        Go to the **You** tab and tap **Account**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Delete account">
        Tap **Delete account**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Type **delete my account**, then tap **Delete account**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If you don't belong to any workspace, PeerNotes opens the welcome screen when you sign in. Select your name on that screen and choose **Account**.

When it's done, you're signed out. Other devices lose access within a few minutes.

## What's deleted and what's kept

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Your account (the name and email address on it, linked Google, Apple, Microsoft, or SSO sign-ins, and credentials) | Deleted right away. Your account is disabled and every session ends. |
| Workspaces where you're the only member and an admin | Deleted with all their content. |
| Your settings in workspaces that remain (such as the name PeerNotes calls you there) | Kept with that workspace. [Email us](mailto:info@peernotes.io) to have them deleted. |
| Content you created in a workspace with other members (thoughts, notes, sources, comments) | Stays in the workspace, shown as **Deleted User** and no longer linked to you. To have it removed, ask that workspace's admin. |
| Backups | Copies may remain in routine backups until they're overwritten on our regular schedule. |
| Analytics | Kept according to our analytics provider's standard schedule. |

If you sign in again later, PeerNotes creates a new, empty account. Your old account can't be restored.

For more detail, see our [Privacy Policy](https://get.peernotes.io/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://get.peernotes.io/terms).

## Ask us to delete your account

If you no longer have the app, or you'd rather we do it, email [info@peernotes.io](mailto:info@peernotes.io):

- Send it from the address you use to sign in.
- Use the subject **Delete my account**.

We verify the request and respond within a reasonable timeframe, in line with applicable law.
