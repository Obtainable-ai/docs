## From a passing thought to team knowledge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capture it">
    Clip a page, dictate a thought, or jot a quick note. Everything lands in your workspace, ready to build on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Develop it together">
    Teammates and AI weigh in right alongside your notes, sharpening the idea together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share it clearly">
    Publish to the shared workspace so everyone's on the same page. Read to catch up, or listen on the go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Clip from anywhere you already read

The browser clipper turns whatever you're reading into a [source](/core-concepts/sources) — Substack, arXiv, Luma, YouTube, Notion, Figma, The Verge, LinkedIn, and anywhere else.

## Core concepts

Everything in PeerNotes flows through three layers, each with its own page:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Sources" icon="box-archive" href="/core-concepts/sources">
    What you read — PDFs, documents, images, videos, and clipped web pages. Private raw material.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Thoughts" icon="lightbulb" href="/core-concepts/thoughts">
    Your reactions, captured in seconds. Private to you, and clustered into [topics](/core-concepts/topics) automatically.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Notes" icon="file-lines" href="/core-concepts/notes">
    Thoughts compiled into structured thinking — developed with peers and AI, then published to the workspace.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
