The Slack integration brings the [Peer Agent](/core-concepts/peer-agent) into Slack. Mention PeerNotes in a channel or send it a direct message, and it replies in the thread. Answers come from your team's [workspace](/core-concepts/workspace), not the open web.

> *@PeerNotes what did we decide about the pricing page last month?*
> — *You settled on two tiers. The reasoning is in the "Pricing review" note, with three customer interviews as sources…*

## Connect Slack

Connecting is **admin-only**. You connect one PeerNotes workspace to one Slack workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Integrations tab">
    Open **Workspace settings** and select **Integrations**. Members don't see this tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add to Slack">
    PeerNotes takes you to Slack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the PeerNotes app">
    Pick your Slack workspace and approve the app. Slack sends you back to PeerNotes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status">
    The Slack card shows **Connected** and your Slack team ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Adding the app to Slack may need approval from a Slack workspace admin, depending on your Slack settings.
</Note>

## Use PeerNotes in Slack

You can reach PeerNotes in two ways:

- **In a channel:** type `@PeerNotes` followed by your question. First add the app to the channel. The app only sees channels it's a member of.
- **In a direct message:** open a DM with the PeerNotes app and ask your question. No mention is needed.

PeerNotes reacts with 👀 when it picks up your message. It then replies in a thread under your message.

### Keep the conversation going

Reply in the same thread to ask a follow-up. PeerNotes also reads the earlier messages in the thread, so it can answer a question about what your teammates just said. It reads up to 50 messages posted since its last reply.

Replies are short: the answer first, then a few supporting lines. Long answers are split across more than one message.

## Who can use it

PeerNotes matches each Slack user to a PeerNotes account by email. To get answers, you need:

- A PeerNotes account with the same email address as your Slack account.
- Membership in the PeerNotes workspace that's connected to Slack.

If either is missing, PeerNotes replies: *"Sorry, I couldn't find you on PeerNotes. Check that your Slack email matches your PeerNotes account and that you're a member of this workspace."*

## What PeerNotes can see

Slack threads can be read by anyone in the channel. Because of this, PeerNotes only answers from content that's **shared with the whole workspace**. It never quotes your private thoughts, notes, or sources in Slack, even when you're the one asking.

See [Sharing and privacy](/features/sharing) for how to share an item with the workspace.

## Manage the connection

Open **Workspace settings → Integrations** to see the Slack card. It shows one of these statuses:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Slack is working. |
| **Needs re-auth** | Slack access expired or changed. Select **Add to Slack** to approve the app again. |
| **Revoked** | The app was removed from Slack, or its access was revoked. Select **Add to Slack** to reconnect. |
| **Not connected** | The workspace has never been connected. |

When Slack is connected, you can also:

- **Reinstall:** run the Slack approval again, for example after Slack asks for new permissions.
- **Disconnect:** select **Disconnect**, then **Save changes**. Until you save, you can select **Undo**.

If something goes wrong during setup, the card shows the error from Slack.
