The PeerNotes team is small but responsive. Here's where to go.

## Feedback, bugs, and roadmap

<Card title="feedback.peernotes.io" icon="bullhorn" href="https://feedback.peernotes.io/">
  The home for everything product: **report a bug**, **request a feature**, **follow the roadmap**, and read **new-release notes**. Vote on other people's requests so we know what matters most.
</Card>

## Account and private questions

<Card title="Email support" icon="envelope">
  For anything account-specific or that you'd rather not post publicly, open a support request from your account settings at [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io). During the research preview, replies may take one to two business days.
</Card>
