PeerNotes is a shared idea and thinking workspace built for teams. Whether you're just hearing about it for the first time or already getting started, the answers below cover the questions we hear most often.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get access to PeerNotes?">
    PeerNotes is in research preview, open to teams. Go to [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io) and choose **Get started** to create your account and set up a workspace. See [get.peernotes.io/download](https://get.peernotes.io/download) for every way to reach your workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is PeerNotes free?">
    While PeerNotes is in research preview, it's free — no card required. Pricing plans for general availability have not been announced yet. Preview users will be notified of any upcoming changes well in advance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What browsers does the extension support?">
    The PeerNotes clipper works in all Chromium-based browsers, including:

    - **Google Chrome**
    - **Microsoft Edge**
    - **Brave**
    - **Arc**
    - **Opera**

    Firefox and Safari support is on the way. Until then, use a Chromium-based browser or clip from the mobile apps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I install the browser extension?">
    Install the PeerNotes clipper from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ojongipbblbjdoidjgmefhdhngmimhbi) — one click, with automatic updates, in any Chromium-based browser. The core workspace needs no extension; it runs in any browser at [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io). See [Install PeerNotes](/getting-started/install-extension) for mobile and AI-assistant options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use PeerNotes without the browser extension?">
    Yes. The core PeerNotes workspace — thoughts, notes, sources, topics, and the AI assistant — is fully accessible at [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io) without the extension. The browser extension adds the web clipper, which lets you save content from any webpage directly into your workspace. If you don't need clipping, you can skip the extension entirely.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the web clipper?">
    The web clipper is a feature built into the PeerNotes browser extension. It lets you highlight text, images, or entire pages from any website and save them directly to your PeerNotes workspace with a single click. Clipped content keeps its source URL so you can always trace it back to the original page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the AI assistant work?">
    Open **Chat** in the sidebar and ask your assistant anything — it answers with your workspace as context, so you can ask about your own thoughts, notes, and sources. Rather than writing for you, it's designed to sharpen your thinking: surfacing the questions a careful reader would ask and resurfacing related thinking you've already captured. Behind the scenes, the PeerNotes agent also organizes your thoughts into topics automatically. See the [AI assistant guide](/features/chat) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share notes with people outside my team?">
    While PeerNotes is in research preview, it's designed as a shared workspace for teams where all members have accounts. Sharing with external collaborators who don't have a PeerNotes account is not currently supported. This may change as the product evolves — share your feedback in [Discord](https://discord.gg/muDmrV2jq7) if this is important to you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I update the browser extension?">
    The Chrome Web Store version updates itself — there's nothing to do. If you're still running an early manual build, reinstall from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ojongipbblbjdoidjgmefhdhngmimhbi) to switch to automatic updates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I get help or report a bug?">
    There are two main ways to get support:

    - **Discord** — Join the community at [discord.gg/muDmrV2jq7](https://discord.gg/muDmrV2jq7) for the fastest response. It's the best place for questions, bug reports, and feedback.
    - **Email support** — Open a support request directly from your account settings at [peernotes.io](https://peernotes.io).

    For a full overview of support options, visit the [contact page](/troubleshooting/contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
